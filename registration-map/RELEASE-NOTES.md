# Registration Coverage Map 1.0.3

Adds product families to the registration list shown when a country is selected.

- New optional **Product family** field mapping. Existing required mappings and the visual identity stay unchanged.
- The **Product families** column shows distinct families from matching source memberships, with up to two lines and full names on hover and in Details.
- Product families are searchable and included in CSV exports. Blank or unmapped values show **Not recorded**.
- Registration counts remain distinct by Registration ID; repeated family, product and catalog rows do not multiply them.

Import this package to replace the existing map, then map your product-family source column to **Product family**. The demo uses fictional data. This remains an uncertified preview.
